Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

SPLOP! is a physics puzzle game developed by Jose Luis Querido Chica. This policy explains what data the app handles and what happens to it. The short version: the app has no servers of its own and your game data stays on your device. The only thing that leaves it is your scores, sent to Apple's Game Center, and only if you are signed in (section 2).

1. Data stored on your device

SPLOP! stores the following in local device storage, and nowhere else:

  • Your best score in each mode and the highest difficulty you have reached.
  • Your settings: music, sound effects, volumes, haptics, language, world, background, piece style, tilt, shake, aiming aid and automatic weapon choice.
  • The songs you have unlocked, the hints you have already seen and whether you chose not to show the intro video.
  • Statistics about your games (score, shots, duration, weapons used), which the game only uses on the device itself.

None of this is sent anywhere: the app has no servers of its own. The only exception is the Game Center scores in section 2. Everything stored on the device is deleted when you uninstall the app.

2. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Apple's gaming service, at the end of each game SPLOP! sends it your score and, in SPLOP! Classic, the difficulty reached. When the game opens, it also sends the best scores already saved on your device. Game Center shows them on the leaderboards with your Game Center nickname, where other players can see them.

SPLOP! does not receive or store this data: Apple handles it under its own privacy policy. If you would rather not appear on the leaderboards, sign out of Game Center in your device Settings: the game works just the same.

3. Motion sensors

If tilt or shake is turned on, the app reads the device's motion sensors to move the pieces while you play. That data is used on the spot, on the device, and is never stored or sent. You can turn both options off in Settings → Controls.

4. Advertising and purchases

This version of SPLOP! has no ads and no in-app purchases. If a future version adds them, this policy will be updated before it is released, with the new date in the header.

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not ask for or store your email, phone number or location.
  • There are no user accounts or passwords of our own (Game Center is an Apple account and is optional).
  • We do not use the device's advertising identifier.

6. Children

SPLOP! is suitable for all audiences. We do not collect personal data from any user, regardless of age. Game Center is optional and run by Apple, with its own parental controls.

7. Your rights

Because the game data lives on your device and under your control, you can exercise your rights directly: settings are visible and editable in the app, and uninstalling it removes everything stored. Scores sent to Game Center belong to your Game Center account and are handled by Apple.

8. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the date in the header will be updated.
