Support

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Something not working, or a question? Write to jlq.software@gmail.com and I will get back to you. For a bug, it helps a lot to know your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS version and the game version (shown at the bottom of Settings and at the end of Help).

How do I play?

Shoot a piece and it splits into two twins; three alike (same size and colour) that touch each other vanish. Keep the pile from crossing the line. In the menu, Help explains the rules and the weapons, and Watch the video shows it in 30 seconds.

What is the difference between SPLOP! Classic and SPLOP! Rain?

Classic is turn based: every shot counts and you progress through difficulties. Rain is real time: balls keep falling and you have to break them before the pile reaches the line.

How do I move the cannon?

Hold the cannon for a moment (a ring fills around it) and drag it anywhere on the board. Away from the walls it shoots in every direction; against a wall, that wall is the limit.

How do I change weapons?

Swipe left or right on the cannon: the selector opens with the weapons you have won. You can also tap an icon in the cannon's row.

What does the Arm button do?

When it is red, the game picks the best weapon for you at every moment. If you pick a weapon yourself, it turns off.

I fired by mistake. How do I cancel a shot?

While aiming, move your finger back to the cannon and release it there: no shot is fired.

The pieces move when I tilt my phone. Can I turn it off?

Yes: in Settings → Controls you can turn tilt and shake off.

Can I start at a higher difficulty?

Yes, in SPLOP! Classic: Settings → Difficulty lists every difficulty you have reached.

Why is everything Halloween?

From 1 October to 7 November, with the world set to "Random every game", the game wears its Halloween theme. To play in another world, pick it in Settings → World.

Is my progress kept if I delete the app?

No. Best scores, difficulties and settings live only on your device, and uninstalling the app deletes them completely. There is no cloud backup and no user account.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language lets you pick Spanish or English. The change is immediate.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. The whole game works offline.

I cannot hear the music or the sound effects.

Check in Settings → Sound that music and effects are on and their volume is up. The game plays even when the iPhone is in silent mode.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. It collects no personal data and has no ads or purchases.
